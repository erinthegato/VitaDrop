VitaDrop

File Transfer

Send files to your Vita

Pick photos, videos or anything from Files.

Add "Send to Vita" to the Share menu

Set this up once (about 5 minutes). After that, send anything with Share → Send to Vita.

Part 1 · Make a new shortcut
  1. Open the Shortcuts app.
  2. Tap + in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap New Shortcut at the top, then Rename. Type Send to Vita and tap Done.
Part 2 · Put it in the Share menu
  1. Tap the ⓘ button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Turn on Show in Share Sheet.
  3. Tap Done.
Part 3 · Handle several files at once
  1. Tap Search for apps and actions at the bottom.
  2. Type repeat with each and tap Repeat with Each.
✓ You should see Repeat with each item in Shortcut Input, then End Repeat. If it says Items instead, tap Items and choose Shortcut Input.
Part 4 · Send each file to the Vita
  1. Search get contents of url and tap Get Contents of URL.
  2. If it appeared under End Repeat, press and hold it and drag it up, between the two Repeat lines.
  3. Tap the blue word URL and type:
    http://vitadrop.local:8080/upload
  4. Tap the small arrow on the right of this action to show more options.
  5. Method: tap GET and choose POST.
  6. Headers: tap Add new header. In Key, type X-File-Name
  7. Tap the Text box next to it. In the bar above the keyboard, tap Repeat Item.
  8. Tap the blue Repeat Item bubble you just added and choose Name.
  9. Request Body: tap JSON and choose File.
  10. Tap the blue word File and choose Repeat Item.
Part 5 · Finish
  1. Search show notification and tap Show Notification. It goes at the bottom, after End Repeat. That's right.
  2. Tap the text Hello World! and change it to Sent to Vita.
  3. Tap Done in the top-right corner.
Your shortcut should look like this

Method: POST
Header: X-File-Name = Repeat Item › Name
Request Body: File = Repeat Item
End Repeat

Try it
  1. Make sure VitaDrop is open on the Vita.
  2. In Photos, open a photo, tap Share, scroll down and tap Send to Vita.
  3. The first time, tap Allow on both questions (connecting, and finding devices on your network).
✓ You get a Sent to Vita notification, and the Vita's file counter goes up.

Not working? Some routers block vitadrop.local. Replace it with the address under the QR code on the Vita and keep upload at the end, for example http://192.168.1.25:8080/upload. If Send to Vita isn't in the Share menu, scroll to the bottom of it, tap Edit Actions and turn it on.
